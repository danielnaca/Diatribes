How it works

You can speak or type in any language — that's fine. The AI always responds according to the mix level you set in the chat options, no matter what you said.

  • Native — The AI responds in plain English. No foreign words at all.
  • Light — The AI responds in English, and the app automatically swaps nouns into French.
  • Medium — Same as Light, but the app swaps whichever word types you've toggled on (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs).
  • Strong — The AI alternates: one sentence in English, the next in French, and so on.
  • Immersion — The AI responds entirely in French, with English hints in brackets for harder words.

On Light and Medium, the French words are inserted by the app — not the AI. Tap any highlighted word to see the English original and save it to your flashcards.
